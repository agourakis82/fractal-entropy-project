# PROSPERO Registration

- **Title:** Fractal Brain Metrics in Giftedness – A Systematic Review and Theoretical Modelling  
- **Review question:** Do gifted individuals (IQ ≥ 120) present higher fractal dimension or entropy in functional brain networks compared with controls?  
- **Searches:** PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science (no date/language limits).  
- **Inclusion criteria:** Human studies, rs‑fMRI or EEG, report fractal metrics (D_H, Hurst, small‑world), provide group means and SD.  
- **Primary outcome:** Standardised mean difference (Hedges g) in D_H.  
- **Secondary outcome:** Shannon entropy, rich‑club coefficient.  
- **Planned analysis:** Random‑effects meta‑analysis (DerSimonian‑Laird); meta‑regression by imaging modality; Bernoulli‑fractal simulation seeded with pooled D_H.  
- **Ethics:** Not required — analysis of fully published, de‑identified data (see §6).  
- **Anticipated completion:** October 2025.
